Content Research
1. Introduction
Content research was conducted to determine the type of information, images, and features required for the Glamour Touch Salon & Makeup Studio website. The purpose of the research is to ensure that the website provides useful information to customers, presents the salon professionally, and makes it easy for visitors to find services and make appointments.
Research into salon websites shows that customers commonly need to see the services offered, prices, examples of previous work, booking options, contact information, operating hours, and the salon's location before deciding whether to make an appointment. A clear and mobile-friendly website can therefore make the customer journey easier.
The content will be organized into the following main website pages:
•	Home
•	About Us
•	Services
•	Gallery
•	Price List
•	Book Appointment
•	Contact Us
Additional content will include promotions, customer testimonials, frequently asked questions, social media links, and salon policies.

19. Conclusion
The content research shows that the Glamour Touch Salon & Makeup Studio website should focus on providing customers with clear, useful, and visually appealing information. The most important content will include the salon's services, prices, photographs, booking options, contact details, location, promotions, and customer reviews.
The research also shows that the website should be designed around a simple customer journey: discover the salon → view services → see examples of the work → check prices → make an appointment. This approach will help the website support the salon's main goals of increasing online bookings, attracting new customers, and providing convenient access to information.
The content will be reviewed and updated by the salon before the website is launched to ensure that all prices, services, contact details, operating hours, promotions, and photographs are accurate.

31. Responsive Breakpoints

The website uses different layouts for different screen sizes.

Desktop

The desktop layout provides a wider content area and supports multi-column sections where appropriate.

Tablet

A breakpoint of approximately 768px was used for tablet-sized screens.

The layout, spacing, navigation, and content widths are adjusted to fit the smaller screen.

Mobile

A breakpoint of approximately 480px was used for mobile devices.

At this breakpoint:

Navigation is adjusted for smaller screens.
Font sizes are reduced where necessary.
Content uses more of the available screen width.
Images resize to fit the screen.
Sections are adjusted to prevent horizontal scrolling.
32. Relative Units

Relative sizing was used where appropriate to improve responsiveness.

The website uses values such as:

%
rem
em

These units allow elements such as fonts, spacing, and containers to adjust more effectively across different screen sizes.

33. Responsive Images

CSS was used to ensure that website images remain responsive.

Images are prevented from becoming wider than their containers and are allowed to resize according to the available screen width.

The responsive image styling helps prevent images from causing horizontal scrolling on smaller devices.

Where appropriate, responsive image techniques such as srcset, sizes, and the picture element can be used to provide suitable image sizes for different devices.
